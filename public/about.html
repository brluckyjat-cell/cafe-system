Our Story & Mission

The Birth of Chai Ceremony

Rooted in the vibrant culture of Jaipur, Chai Ceremony Cafe was founded with a passion to blend traditional Rajasthani royal hospitality with modern artisanal cafe dining. We believe tea is not just a beverage—it is an emotion, a ritual, and a celebration of togetherness.

Every single cup of our signature Kulhad Chai is brewed using hand-crushed spices, fresh herbs, and rich farm milk, served in earthen clay pots that capture the true fragrance of Rajasthan's soil.
